# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in d-raft a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race,
religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

- Demonstrate empathy and kindness.
- Respect differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the community and the integrity of the research.
- Take responsibility, apologize, and learn from mistakes.

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate intimidation, sexualized attention,
and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
privately to the project maintainer through the contact options on
[aminkbi's GitHub profile](https://github.com/aminkbi). All reports will be
reviewed promptly and fairly, with respect for the reporter's privacy and
safety. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may
temporarily or permanently restrict participation when appropriate.
